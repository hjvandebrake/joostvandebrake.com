Use it responsibly

A conversation starter, not an employment decision

This is an evidence-informed reflection tool, not a validated psychological, medical, or occupational-health diagnostic. It does not predict individual performance or wellbeing and should not be used for selection, appraisal, or other employment decisions. One person's answers cannot represent an entire team or organization.

Use the result alongside conversations with team members, team leaders, and other relevant information about the work.
