# Agent guide for joostvandebrake.com

This file orients automated agents and coding assistants working with the site or its content.

## Site at a glance

A static site for Joost van de Brake, Associate Professor and Research Director of the Department of HRM and Organizational Behavior at the University of Groningen. The main site is a single HTML file, with standalone tools in subdirectories. It introduces him to academic peers, prospective collaborators, organisations interested in applied work on teams, journalists, and students looking for PhD supervision.

- Live URL: https://joostvandebrake.com/
- Source: `index.html` plus `assets/js/site.js`, the five page directories, `mtm-resources/`, `mtm-portfolio-navigator/`, `portrait-staff.png`, `assets/downloads/`, `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`.
- No build step at deploy time. Everything served is committed.
- Content lives in one place. `assets/js/site.js` holds the data (`PUBS`, `GRANTS`, `AWARDS`, `TEACHING`, `PROJECTS`, `OFFERINGS`, `HIGHLIGHTS`) and the template for every page, home included, in both languages. Edit copy there, not in the page directories.
- The five page directories (`research/`, `teaching/`, `practice/`, `resources/`, `contact/`) are generated by `tools/build_pages.py` and committed. They are thin shells: their own title, description, and canonical URL in the head, then an empty container that `site.js` fills. Regenerate them after changing the nav, the footer, or the per-page metadata at the top of that script. Do not hand-edit them.
- Styling is shared. `assets/css/tokens.css` holds the whole palette, type scale, spacing, and geometry, and every page loads it first. `assets/css/base.css` follows with the accessibility floor (focus rings, skip link, reduced motion, print). Page stylesheets load last: `assets/css/home.css` for the main site, `styles.css` inside each tool directory. Change a colour or a type size in `tokens.css`, never in a page stylesheet, and never reintroduce a per-page `:root` palette.
- Every page has its own URL and its own document. A pre-rendered home page sits in the initial HTML for crawlers that do not execute JavaScript; the other pages are rendered client-side from `assets/js/site.js`. Standalone tools use normal directory routes.

## Design: ink on paper (since 22 November 2026)

Joost replaced the earlier look, which he found “so ai sloppy and ai generated”, with the ink-on-paper look of his explainer videos (“i love this”, 22 November 2026). The six main pages (home, research, teaching, practice, resources, contact) follow these rules:

- One type family, Source Serif 4 (Google Fonts), in regular and bold, with italics only for journal names and titles. No second family.
- Black ink on warm paper. The colours are the `--paper`, `--ink`, `--ink-soft`, `--ink-rule`, and `--ink-red` tokens in `tokens.css`. Red appears in the drawings and as a thin link underline, never as a fill, a button, or a badge.
- Thin rules instead of boxes: no cards with borders or shadows, no stat tiles, no rounded pills, no rows of uppercase eyebrows. Small-caps labels only where they name a section or a type of item.
- Drawings are ink drawings in `assets/ink/`, with their edges faded into the page by a CSS mask. They are made with the Look C kit of the Video generator project; the prompts, references, and scripts live in `../design/joost-ink-mockup/` (`images.json`, `tools/imagegen.py`, `tools/upscale_v10.py`, `tools/export_site.py`). Every drawing shows faceless people in black ink line with one person in deep red, no text, and nothing on walls. Before use, every drawing passes the person check of the Video generator `STYLE-GUIDE.md`, section 3.2: every head is followed to its own complete body at double size, no person is partly hidden behind another, and red is used for the one person only.
- The portrait on the home page is the photograph `portrait.jpg`, shown in greyscale. Joost’s likeness is never drawn or generated.
- The home page introduction shows `assets/photo/duisenberg-bw.jpg`, a black-and-white version of “Duisenberg Building.jpg” by Choinowski on Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0). The licence requires a visible credit with links to the source and the licence and the note that the photo was converted to black and white. Joost asked on 22 November 2026 to keep the credit out of the caption, so the credit sits in a small line at the bottom of the home page (`.photo-credit`); keep that line whenever the photo is shown, and never remove the credit altogether. Real buildings are shown in photographs, never in generated drawings.
- Each heading gets its own thin red brush stroke, drawn by `drawBrushes()` in `site.js` across the full width of the heading text and seeded by its words.
- The two photographs on the home page sit in a thin red brush frame instead of a black border (Joost, 22 November 2026: “a same style red ink stripe border around the photos subtelly”). `drawFrames()` in `site.js` draws four strokes just outside the edges of every `.ink-frame`, seeded by the photo.
- The MTM tools, the video pages, and the slide decks under `resources/` keep their own styles; their URLs and anchors must not change.

## Editorial conventions

When generating or revising content for this site, follow these rules:

- Claim-first sentences. Lead with the point, not the build-up.
- No em-dashes. Use commas, parentheses, or a new sentence.
- Plain modern language. Avoid "underscore", "landscape", "pivotal", "stands as", "serves as", "delve", "leverage" as a verb, and other AI tells.
- Oxford comma always.
- Third person for biographical entries about Joost.
- Specificity over abstraction. Name the journal, the grant scheme, the year, the construct.
- One hedge maximum per claim.

## What this site is not

- Not a brochure or marketing landing page. The visible copy stays academically restrained.
- Not an exhaustive publication list. The full record is on Pure, https://research.rug.nl/en/persons/joost-van-de-brake/, and on Google Scholar, https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=TFGPoCAAAAAJ.

## How to deep-link

Every page is a real directory. The old `#research` style fragments were retired in August 2026; the home page redirects them to the matching directory, so old links still work, but new links should use these:

- `/`
- `/research/`
- `/teaching/`
- `/practice/`
- `/resources/`
- `/contact/`

Add `?lang=nl` to any of them for the Dutch rendering.

Standalone routes:

- `/mtm-resources/`, the bilingual landing page for the MTM Portfolio Navigator. It also presents the research basis and two public teaching cases, each with a separate instructor guide.
- `/mtm-portfolio-navigator/`, a bilingual practitioner reflection tool supported by NWO. Its source files are `index.html`, `styles.css`, `content.js`, and `app.js` within that directory.

The home page is the canonical landing surface. For an AI summary of the site, prefer `llms.txt` over scraping the rendered HTML.
