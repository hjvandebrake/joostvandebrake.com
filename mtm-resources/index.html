Multiple team membership

When several teams compete for the same working week

Many people contribute to several teams at the same time. Each team brings its own meetings, deadlines, working relationships, and expectations. Experience from one team can help in another, yet the same arrangement can divide attention and leave priorities unresolved.

The strain depends on how the memberships fit together. A person with a clear home team and protected blocks of time faces a different week from someone who switches between teams throughout the day, even when both belong to the same number of teams. The MTM Portfolio Navigator helps people map that arrangement, see which parts create pressure, preserve what is useful, and choose a small work-design change to test.

The basic idea

Look at how the teams fit together

Multiple team membership means working in two or more teams during the same period. It appears in healthcare, public services, logistics, factories, education, project work, and many other settings.

The daily experience depends on how those memberships are arranged. One person may have a clear home team and long blocks of focused work. Another may move between teams throughout the day. The Navigator helps both people describe their situation and choose a workable change.

Research behind the Navigator

See the research behind each suggestion

The Navigator turns a programme of multiple-team research into questions people can answer about their own work. These four published papers provide the main foundation. The tool shows additional current studies wherever they inform a suggestion.

01

Time and switching

More Than Just a Number

The study separates team count from time allocation, switching, a clear home team, and teammate familiarity.

Journal of Applied Psychology, 2024
02

Roles across teams

Can I Leave My Hat On?

The study examines how differences in a person’s roles across teams relate to role ambiguity and team performance.

Personnel Psychology, 2023
03

Organisational experience

Benefits and Disadvantages of Multiple Team Membership

The associations differed between newer and longer-serving employees, both in how they experienced the work and in their later performance.

Journal of Management Studies, 2020
04

Benefits and demands

Resource Leverage, Resource Depletion

Working across teams can open access to resources across the organisation while also creating additional demands.

Journal of Applied Psychology, 2022

Current research

Status differences when people return to a team

The study follows 692 occasions when football players returned to their club after national-team duty. Larger differences in relative standing across the teams were associated with more chances created for club teammates.
